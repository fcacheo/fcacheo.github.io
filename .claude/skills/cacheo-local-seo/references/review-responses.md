# Review Response Playbook — Cacheo Insurance Agency

Response rate and response speed are GBP prominence signals. They are also the
only part of the review system the agency controls. Target: every review
answered within 48 hours, no exceptions for negative ones.

## The compliance rules that override everything below

An insurance agency is not a restaurant. These are not style preferences —
breaking them creates real exposure:

1. **Never confirm or deny that a reviewer is a customer.** Publicly tying a
   named person to a policy is a privacy problem regardless of who posted
   first. "Thank you for the kind words" — not "thank you for trusting us with
   your auto policy since 2019."
2. **Never discuss a claim, a premium, a policy number, or a coverage decision
   in public.** Move it to phone on the first reply, every time.
3. **Never give coverage advice in a review response.** No "you should have had
   umbrella coverage." That is advice given without an application, in writing,
   to an audience.
4. **Never blame the carrier, the adjuster, or the customer.** Farmers brand
   rules aside, it reads badly to the actual audience — the prospect reading
   reviews six months from now.
5. **Do not offer compensation, discounts, or free anything** to get a review
   changed. Review-gating and incentives violate Google's policy and can pull
   the whole review set.

The audience for a negative-review response is never the reviewer. It is the
next prospect deciding whether to call. Write for that person.

## Structure

**Positive (4–5 star):** thank → name the human who did the work → one
specific, non-identifying detail → light door-open. Three sentences. Do not
keyword-stuff; "Long Beach auto insurance agent" jammed into a thank-you reads
like a bot and Google does not reward it.

**Negative (1–3 star):** acknowledge → no excuses, no counter-story → take it
offline with a real name and number → stop. Four sentences maximum. Never
argue a fact publicly even when the reviewer is wrong about it.

**Non-customer / mistaken-identity review:** acknowledge you cannot find a
record, give the contact path, and file for removal through GBP if it is
clearly a different agency. Do not accuse the reviewer of lying.

## Templates

Rotate wording. Ten identical thank-yous in a row reads as automated to both
readers and Google.

### Positive — general
> Thank you for taking the time to write this, {first_name}. I will pass this
> along to {team_member}, who will be glad to hear it. We are here whenever you
> need us — (310) 734-6151.

### Positive — names a team member
> {team_member} is exactly the person we hoped you would get. Thank you for the
> kind words, {first_name}. Give us a call anytime at (310) 734-6151.

### Positive — mentions responsiveness / speed
> Getting back to people quickly is the part of this job we care most about, so
> this means a lot. Thank you, {first_name}.

### Positive — Spanish
> Muchas gracias por sus palabras, {first_name}. Se las voy a compartir a
> {team_member}. Estamos a sus órdenes cuando nos necesite — (310) 734-6151.

### Negative — service complaint
> Thank you for telling us this, {first_name} — I would rather hear it than
> not. This is not the experience we want anyone to have. I would like to
> understand what happened; please call me directly at (310) 734-6151 and ask
> for Francisco.

### Negative — about a claim or a coverage outcome
> I am sorry you are dealing with this. Claims questions are not something I
> can go into here, but I do want to help you get answers. Please call the
> office at (310) 734-6151 and ask for Francisco.

*(Note what this does not say: it does not confirm a claim exists, does not
name the carrier's decision, and does not apologize for an outcome the agency
may not control.)*

### Negative — about price
> I understand — rates across California have moved a lot, and that is
> frustrating. What I can do is sit down and go through the coverage line by
> line to make sure you are not paying for something you do not need. Call
> (310) 734-6151 and ask for Francisco.

### Negative — Spanish
> Gracias por decírnoslo, {first_name}. No es la experiencia que queremos que
> nadie tenga. Me gustaría entender qué pasó — por favor llame directamente al
> (310) 734-6151 y pregunte por Francisco.

### Mistaken identity / no record
> Thank you for the feedback. I am not finding a record on our side, and I want
> to make sure this reached the right office. Please call (310) 734-6151 so we
> can sort it out.

## Before publishing any response

- [ ] No policy, claim, premium, or coverage detail named
- [ ] Reviewer's customer status neither confirmed nor denied
- [ ] No advice about what coverage anyone should carry
- [ ] Nobody blamed — not carrier, adjuster, or reviewer
- [ ] Nothing offered in exchange for changing the review
- [ ] Phone number correct: (310) 734-6151
- [ ] Wording not copied verbatim from the last response posted
